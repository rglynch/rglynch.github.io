Richard G. Lynch, Ph.D.

I build AI-assisted systems and the validation around them

Ph.D. in Mathematics from the University of Missouri-Columbia. My research was in frame theory and compressive sensing. After five years teaching at Texas A&M, I moved into educational technology, spending 2021 to 2026 at Edfinity building and maintaining the technical systems behind an online assessment platform.

These days I work on AI-assisted systems and the numerical and validation machinery around them, and I am drawn to work that stays close to where it actually gets used. This site has my research, background, and CV.
