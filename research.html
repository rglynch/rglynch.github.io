Research Background

My dissertation was on subsequences of frames and their operators. Frames are overcomplete systems of vectors that generalize orthonormal bases. The overcompleteness makes them more robust to noise and erasure, which is why they show up in signal processing and coding theory.

My work also covers compressive sensing: recovering sparse signals from far fewer measurements than classical sampling theory requires. This connects to ℓ₁ minimization, restricted isometry properties, and random matrix theory. Several of my papers sit at the overlap between these two areas.

Core topics

Frame Theory Compressive Sensing Sparse Signal Recovery ℓ₁ Minimization / LASSO Random Matrix Theory Semidefinite Programming Numerical Linear Algebra Low-Rank Matrix Recovery Harmonic Analysis Quantization

Applications

Frame theory and compressive sensing come up in MRI acceleration, medical imaging reconstruction, recommendation systems, and quantized machine learning. In inverse problems more broadly, sparsity-based approaches are increasingly being compared to or replaced by generative model priors.

Selected Publications

  • Preserving Injectivity under Subgaussian Mappings and Its Application to Compressed Sensing
    P.G. Casazza, X. Chen, and R.G. Lynch — Applied and Computational Harmonic Analysis, 49(2), 451–470, 2020
    Recovery guarantees for dictionary-sparse signals via ℓ₁-synthesis. Gaussian widths, null space properties, subgaussian measurement design.
  • Recovering Low-Rank Matrices from Binary Measurements
    S. Foucart and R.G. Lynch — Inverse Problems and Imaging, 13(4), 703–720, 2019
    Low-rank matrix recovery from 1-bit quantized measurements. SVD thresholding, semidefinite programming, restricted isometry properties. Connects to recommendation systems, quantized ML, and signal processing. Referenced in 2024 tensor recovery work.
  • A Brief Introduction to Hilbert Space Frame Theory and Its Applications
    P.G. Casazza and R.G. Lynch — in Finite Frame Theory: A Complete Introduction to Overcompleteness, Proc. Symposia in Applied Mathematics, vol. 73, AMS, 1–52, 2016
    Invited 52-page survey chapter covering the foundations of frame theory for researchers entering the field.
  • Weaving Frames
    T. Bemrose, P.G. Casazza, K. Gröchenig, M.C. Lammers, and R.G. Lynch — Operators and Matrices, 10(4), 1093–1116, 2016
    Introduced the concept of weaving frames, a new subfield in frame theory with applications in distributed signal processing and wireless sensor networks.
